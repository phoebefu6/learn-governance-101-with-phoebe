# learn governance 101 with phoebe

**Data & AI Governance, from scratch** - the plain-English on-ramp to the whole governance shelf, by Phoebe Fu.

> 🌱 **The on-ramp, not the law library. Educational, not legal advice.** It teaches the vocabulary and the map, then points you at the deeper courses (PDPA, GDPR, the EU AI Act, and data governance) for the detail.

Every headline about a data breach, a consent banner, a cookie pop-up, or the EU AI Act uses words most people were never taught. This course is the plain-English door into all of it - no law degree, no acronym soup. In six short sessions you learn what personal data is, what governance means and why it matters, the big regimes at a glance, what a breach is, and what a DPO actually does. It is the foundation the four deeper governance courses build on.

## One track, 6 sessions (45 min each)

1. **What is data, and why govern it** - personal data vs data, the one test to spot it, and the three reasons governance exists: trust, risk, law.
2. **The vocabulary, in one breath** - consent, purpose, data subject, controller vs processor, and the rights people have.
3. **The regime map + which touches me** - PDPA, GDPR, and the EU AI Act at a glance, plus a live "which regime touches me?" chooser.
4. **When it goes wrong: breaches** - what a breach is, the first moves, and what "notify within 72 hours" means in plain terms.
5. **Who owns this: the DPO + roles** - what a Data Protection Officer does, controller vs processor, and governance as an everyday habit.
6. **Your next steps** - how the four deeper courses build on this, a five-step starting plan, and the chooser revisited.

## The live chooser

Session 3 (and the homepage) carry a small in-browser **"which regime touches me?"** chooser: answer four plain-English yes/no questions and it maps you to the regimes that likely apply - PDPA, the DNC rules, GDPR, and the EU AI Act - and points you at the deeper course for each. A teaching map, not a legal determination.
