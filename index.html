Data & AI Governance, from scratch

Every headline about a data breach, a consent banner, a cookie pop-up, or the EU AI Act uses words most people were never taught. This is the plain-English door into all of it - no law degree, no acronym soup. In six short sessions you will learn what personal data is, what governance means and why it matters, the big regimes at a glance (PDPA, GDPR, the EU AI Act), what a breach is, and what a DPO actually does. It is the foundation the four deeper governance courses build on - start here, then go as deep as you need.
