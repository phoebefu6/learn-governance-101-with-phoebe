# Official course map - learn-governance-101-with-phoebe

**Course:** Data & AI Governance, from scratch (the diff-1 on-ramp for the gov shelf).
**Audience:** brand-new to governance - a manager, a founder, a freshly-appointed DPO. Plain English, no law-heavy detail.
**Bucket:** gov · **diff:** 1 (foundational) · **audience tag:** both · single leader-voice concept track, 6 x ~45 min.

## The source universe (inverted learn-loop)

Unlike a normal build, this on-ramp is not learned from new vendor courses. Phoebe has already
**built and verified the four deeper gov-bucket courses**, so those are the canonical sources - this
course distills *down* from them into a first door. The verification job here is the reverse of
usual: check that each simplification does **not distort** what the deeper courses teach.

Canonical sources (her own, already source-verified):
- `learn-data-governance-with-phoebe` (PDPA-first governance, DPO + exec tracks, Himalaya case)
- `learn-pdpa-dnc-with-phoebe` (Singapore PDPA ten obligations + Do Not Call, Marina Retail case)
- `learn-gdpr-with-phoebe` (GDPR for a SG org - Art 3 reach, lawful bases, rights, 72-hour breach)
- `learn-ai-governance-with-phoebe` (EU AI Act risk tiers, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, Vantage case)

Primary regulators, for the at-a-glance facts only (not deep dive):
- PDPC Singapore (pdpc.gov.sg) - PDPA + DNC
- European Commission / EUR-Lex - GDPR (Reg (EU) 2016/679) and the EU AI Act (Reg (EU) 2024/1689)

**Educational, not legal advice** appears on every page. This is a map, not a manual.

## The 80% bar for an on-ramp

An on-ramp does not teach 80% of the deeper material - it teaches 100% of the *vocabulary and mental
model* a beginner needs to then take the deeper courses. Coverage is measured as: after this course,
can a beginner read a headline about a breach, a consent banner, or the EU AI Act and know what the
words mean and whether it touches them? Each session ends by pointing at the deeper course that
continues it.

## Not covered by design (say so on the page)

- No statutory detail, section numbers, or case law - the deeper courses carry those.
- No jurisdiction beyond Singapore (PDPA/DNC) and the EU (GDPR/AI Act) at a glance; other regimes
  (CCPA, PIPL, etc.) are named as "there are many more" but not taught.
- No hands-on compliance artifacts (no DPIA/FRIA templates, no consent-form drafting) - that is the
  practitioner work in the deeper courses.
- Not legal advice; the chooser is a teaching map, not a determination.

## Verification note (re-check before delivery)

Fast-moving items to re-verify against the deeper courses + regulators before any refresh:
- GDPR breach notification = **72 hours** to the supervisory authority (Art 33). ✓
- EU AI Act = **Reg (EU) 2024/1689**, risk-tiered, extraterritorial by output. ✓
- PDPA breach notification = notify PDPC + affected individuals when a breach is **likely to result in
  significant harm** or is of **significant scale** (>=500 individuals). ✓ (keep in plain terms on a4)
- DNC = part of the PDPA regime, separate registers; ongoing-relationship + consent exceptions exist. ✓
